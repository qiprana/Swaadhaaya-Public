How sources are verified, and why numbering can differ

A source location is not the same as a fully transcribed śloka. We record the work, section, source edition, explicit reference, passage scope, and review status. For Bhāvaprakāśa, the SanskritDocuments electronic text numbers the Amalaki passage 38–40, while a NIIMH-derived index maps it to 36–38. We must preserve edition-specific IDs.
